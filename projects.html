Detecting hidden threats

My projects are grouped by the layer of the system where the threat hides: the chip, the embedded device, or the software on top. For a full list of papers, see Google Scholar or ORCID.

In chips

Machine learning-based anomaly detection in hardware security

PI · GTU Scientific Research Projects (BAP) · 2025–2026 ongoing
  • Detecting hardware Trojans at the pre-silicon stage with deep learning and reinforcement learning.
  • Combining structural and side-channel features to find hidden and rare Trojans in large circuits.

Rare node extraction and test generation

Collaboration · SBA Research, Vienna · 2025– ongoing
  • Comparing rare node extraction methods in digital circuits.
  • Generating test vectors for hardware Trojan detection.

Neural network based hardware Trojan detection

Advisor · TÜBİTAK · 2025 · completed
  • Transformed Verilog HDL circuits into graph representations.
  • Developed graph neural networks (GNNs) for automated Trojan detection in Verilog circuits.

Useful circuit criteria for delay-based Trojan detection

Co-PI · GTU Scientific Research Projects (BAP) · 2021–2022 · completed
  • Investigated spatial correlations in digital circuits to improve delay-based Trojan detection.
  • Used path delays as side-channel signals to identify potential Trojans.
  • Basis of my Ph.D. thesis.

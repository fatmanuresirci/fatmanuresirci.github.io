Work with me

Join the hunt

I am looking for motivated students who want to work on the security of computing systems. Undergraduate and graduate students are welcome.

Possible topics

In chips

Hardware Trojan detection, side-channel analysis, test generation.

In embedded devices

Fault injection attacks, threat detection in embedded processors.

In software & communication

Vulnerability detection, phishing and vishing defence.

What helps

  • SkillsBasic programming in Python or C, or digital design with Verilog. You do not need all of them.
  • AttitudeCuriosity, patience with experiments, and willingness to read papers.

How to apply

  • Step 1Send an email to fesirci@gtu.edu.tr with the subject [Research] Your name.
  • Step 2Attach your CV, and write a few sentences on which topic interests you and why.
  • Step 3If it is a good fit, I will send you a short task to get started.

About

I am a computer engineer and security researcher at Gebze Technical University, where I received my Ph.D. in 2024. My research focuses on finding threats hidden in computing systems, from chips and embedded devices to software. I combine experimental hardware work, statistical analysis and machine learning. I was a visiting researcher at IMT Atlantique (France) and SBA Research (Vienna). I work towards computing systems we can trust, from the first transistor to the last line of code. I am always happy to hear from students and collaborators who share this curiosity.

Hidden threats

Hardware Trojans, side-channel analysis, test generation and learning-based detection.

In software & communication
